Membership Categories

Three ways to belong.

Student Member

For current students, while you're still here.

  • Access to mentorship matching
  • Eligible for scholarships and bootcamps
  • Free entry to student events
  • Automatic alumni listing upon graduation
Join as a Student
Most Common

Graduate Member

For every NACOS UNN graduate, free to join.

  • Full alumni directory and networking access
  • Invitations to reunions and regional meetups
  • Mentor or be mentored, either direction
  • Voting rights on community initiatives
Join as a Graduate

Financial Member

For graduates ready to fund what comes next.

  • Everything in Graduate Membership
  • A direct say in project funding decisions
  • Named recognition in annual reports
  • Early access to new initiatives
Become a Financial Member

Questions people actually ask.

Any graduate of the NACOS UNN Computer Science programme can join as a Graduate or Financial Member. Current students can join as Student Members while still enrolled.

Graduate Membership is free. Financial Membership is a recurring contribution you set yourself — there's no fixed minimum, and you can change or pause it at any time.
