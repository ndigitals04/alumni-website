Our Story

NACOS UNN Alumni wasn't started by an institution — it was started by graduates who kept noticing the same gap: a department that shaped their careers, and a generation of students coming up without the support they once needed too. We exist to make sure that support is no longer optional or occasional, but organized, funded, and built to outlast any one set of graduates.

The Challenge

What we kept running into, over and over.

A department stretched thin

The Computer Science department at UNN has kept producing strong graduates for decades — but its facilities and staffing haven't scaled with demand. Lecture halls fill beyond capacity, and faculty stretch across far more students than they were resourced for.

Equipment that's aged out

Labs still run on hardware several generations behind what employers expect graduates to already know. Without sustained investment, the gap between what's taught and what's needed in industry keeps widening every year.

A widening readiness gap

Strong fundamentals don't automatically translate into job readiness. Many students reach graduation having never seen a real industry codebase, a technical interview, or the soft skills that come from structured mentorship.

Thousands of graduates, no shared network

Alumni scattered into successful, disconnected careers, with no organized way to find each other, give back, or even hear what the department needed. Goodwill existed everywhere — it just had nowhere to go.

One-off help isn't enough

Individual alumni had already been donating, mentoring, and hiring informally for years. What was missing wasn't goodwill — it was a sustainable structure that could turn scattered generosity into something the department could actually plan around.

Vision

A NACOS UNN where every graduate stays connected, every student is supported, and the department's legacy compounds with each generation that passes through it.

Mission

To unite NACOS UNN graduates in advancing student success, strengthening departmental infrastructure, and building sustainable systems of mentorship and giving that outlast any single generation.

Core Objectives

Seven objectives. One coordinated effort.

Department Development

Funding the facilities and tools the department needs to stay competitive.

Student Support

Scholarships and resources for students who need them most.

Mentorship

Pairing current students with alumni who've already walked the path.

Career Growth

Industry training, certifications, and real pathways into employment.

Alumni Unity

One organized network instead of thousands of disconnected graduates.

Sustainable Giving

Structured, recurring support the department can actually plan around.

Long-term Legacy

Building systems that outlast any single set of graduates.

Long-Term Vision
