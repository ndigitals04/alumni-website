Building Today's Alumni. Empowering Tomorrow's Leaders.

Why We Exist

A department full of graduates, and a network that never formed.

Every year, another set of graduates walks out of the Computer Science department at UNN and into the world. Some land roles at global tech companies. Others build startups, lead engineering teams, or shape policy. But for years, that success stayed scattered — thousands of graduates, all moving forward, with almost no thread connecting them back to Nsukka.

Meanwhile, the students coming up behind them were navigating the same path largely alone: figuring out internships by guesswork, learning industry tools the department couldn't always afford to teach, and rarely meeting anyone who had already done what they were trying to do.

NACOS UNN Alumni exists to close that gap — to turn a department of disconnected success stories into a single, organized community that funds, mentors, and builds for the students who'll wear the same name next.

Our Mission

Three commitments guide everything we build.

Build Community

Reconnect graduates across every set, country, and career path into one organized, active alumni network.

Support Students

Fund scholarships, mentorship, and career resources for current students who need them most.

Transform the Department

Invest directly in the infrastructure, tools, and recognition that keep the department competitive.

1. Student

2. Graduate

3. Career Success

Builds a thriving career in tech.
